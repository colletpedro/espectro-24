Wow, this movie was actually boring AF. Didn’t feel like a Nolan movie at all. I rarely give low scores, and it hurts me to do it to a Nolan movie, but man. It took me forever to get through this one. All of his other movies, epic, love them. 

Great acting by all, don’t get me wrong, but also too many famous faces for no reason, roles wasted for a couple of lines. Felt like a bunch of them literally just wanted to be in a Nolan movie, so he gave them all such minor parts 

The detonation and aftermath sequences were good
