### Índice 31 (nota 2.0★)

Spooktober 2024: Day 25 watching a horror movie Truly sorry, folks, but I seriously don’t get the hype for this. Throughout it's protracted runtime, i found Cure excruciatingly overstays it's welcome, by spinning out in a mystery that leads nowhere significant. To be fair, the premise itself is thoroughly intriguing: a killer feigning amnesia, endowed with a disquieting aura and a hypnotic prowess that allows him to tap into the subconscious of others, compelling them to commit heinous acts of violence without their cognizance. Such concept is actually rife with potential for exploration into the sinister recesses of human psychology. Yet, alas, the execution is agonizingly elongated, dragging it's feet through a mire of monotony that offers neither excitement nor genuine intrigue. Instead of gripping suspense or tension, we’re given repetitive interrogations and a mystery that barely unfolds, with Detective Takabe going in circles without ever yielding anything fruitful. There's also hardly any dramatic tension from Takabe’s personal family life, which could have added much-needed stakes, yet it's barely developed. As for Cure being labeled as “horror” is just wildly exaggerated & preposterous. There is scarcely any signs of the ominous, the horrific, let alone the thrills to be found within it's frames. Sure, it leans into police procedural thriller territory, but unlike other renowned crime films that takes advantage of dark atmospheres, intense thrills and menacing villain, yet Cure chooses instead to meander, devoid of the tension that one might reasonably expect. As for the ending, it is just okay at best, or perhaps an anticlimactic denouement to an otherwise excruciatingly drawn-out narrative. Overall, for lack of a better word, Cure is basically just nothing burger, and a snoozefest, wrapped up in an overrated hype that it doesn’t live up to. In the end, I confess I am at a loss to understand it's appeal, and I find myself utterly indifferent to the exalted status it has amassed within cinematic discourse. Perhaps some among you might wish to send me to the guillotine for this audacious opinion, so i’ll evade for a bit!🤐 However, i'll definitely be back soon after my rewatch for a review from a movie what i consider a truly profound horror experience, that is The Substance (2024), to cleanse my palate and to appropriately conclude this Halloween season.
