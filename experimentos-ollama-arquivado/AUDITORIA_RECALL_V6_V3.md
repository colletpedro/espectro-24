### Índice 31 (nota 2.0★)

Spooktober 2024: Day 25 watching a horror movie Truly sorry, folks, but I seriously don’t get the hype for this. Throughout it's protracted runtime, i found Cure excruciatingly overstays it's welcome, by spinning out in a mystery that leads nowhere significant.
